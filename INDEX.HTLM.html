WHAT MAKES YOU FEEL APPRECIATED AT WORK?

Choose one response to each of the 8 workplace situations. There are no right or wrong answers. At the end, you will see which forms of appreciation you currently prefer.

Discuss: Would your preferred way of receiving appreciation be the same in every situation or organization? How could your manager find out your preferences?

This is an educational reflection activity inspired by the workplace appreciation framework, not a validated psychological assessment. Answers remain on this device and are not submitted or stored.
